I'm a Senior Engineering Manager at [Sentry](https://sentry.io), where I work on [Seer](https://sentry.io/product/seer/), Sentry's AI debugger. Previously I led Session Replay, bootstrapped Sentry's gaming team, launched [console support](https://blog.sentry.io/error-monitoring-and-crash-reporting-for-gaming-consoles/), and managed SDK teams. I joined Sentry relatively early — when the whole company was the size of today's SDK team — to build the [.NET SDK](https://github.com/getsentry/sentry-dotnet) after over a decade of writing C#. I still have a soft spot for working on open source projects like [NuGet Trends](https://nugettrends.com) and [Code Review Trends](https://codereviewtrends.com).

I've called five countries home, and with friends and family left behind with each move, they always will be. I travel for history, culture and people, but also to dive and sail. And I love maps, so I [made one](/travel/) for trips down memory lane.
